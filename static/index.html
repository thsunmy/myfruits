✳ FRESH FROM MALAYSIA

The local line-up.

Five familiar favourites, each with a flavour and story all its own.

01 / 05 SWEET & JUICY

THE HAIRY ONE

Rambutan

Don't let its spiky red coat fool you. Inside is translucent, juicy flesh with a gently sweet flavour.

✳ Peel away the skin to enjoy

02 / 05 SWEET & TANGY

THE PURPLE ONE

Mangosteen

Crack open its deep purple rind to find soft white segments that balance sweetness with a little tang.

✳ Known as the queen of fruits

03 / 05 RICH & CREAMY

THE BOLD ONE

Durian

Famous for its powerful aroma, durian has custardy flesh and a rich flavour that inspires strong opinions.

✳ Known as the king of fruits

04 / 05 CRISP & REFRESHING

THE STARRY ONE

Starfruit

Also called belimbing, this crisp fruit is sweet to tart and makes a lovely star shape when sliced.

✳ Delicious sliced fresh

05 / 05 SOFT & MELLOW

THE SUNNY ONE

Papaya

With its vibrant orange flesh and mellow sweetness, ripe papaya is a refreshing everyday favourite.

✳ Scoop out the seeds and enjoy
